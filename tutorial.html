Welcome aboard

Every border you've crossed, stamped in your own passport.

Modern passports rarely get stamped anymore, so Stratus fills that void — search any country, state, landmark, or city and turn it into a personalized ink stamp, laid out across pages just like a real passport. No accounts, no backend, nothing to sync. Here's the whole thing in about three minutes.

Search a place Design your stamp Your passport pages Stats Add manually Backup & settings
  1. 1

    Search a place

    Type a country, state, city, or landmark into the search bar — Stratus looks it up with OpenStreetMap and shows matches with their full location so you pick the right one.

    Search needs no account or API key; it queries the free, public OpenStreetMap Nominatim service directly from your browser.

  2. 2

    Design your stamp

    Pick an ink color and a shape — classic circle, scalloped, or a visa-style rectangle — set the date you visited, and jot a short note. The preview updates live so you see exactly what lands on the page.

    🖋️ 6 ink colors ⬭ 3 shapes
  3. 3

    Flip through your passport pages

    Saved stamps fill a passport page, newest first, six to a page — just like the real thing. Use the page arrows to flip back through your travel history, and tap any stamp to edit or remove it.

  4. 4

    Track your stats

    The header above your passport tallies total stamps, countries visited, and continents covered — continent data is looked up automatically the first time you stamp a country.

  5. 5

    Add a place manually

    Can't find somewhere, or want to work offline? Hit Add Manually and type in the name, type, and country code yourself — the stamp designer works exactly the same either way.

  6. 6

    Back up your passport

    Stratus stores everything only in this browser's local storage. Use Export JSON in Settings regularly to save a backup, and Import JSON to restore it anywhere. Settings also has day, twilight, and night atmospheres to match your sky.

That's the whole app.
